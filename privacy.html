Privacy Policy

Hasanabi Central Uploader · Effective November 11, 2026

Summary

Hasanabi Central Uploader ("the app") is a private tool that posts Hasanabi Central's own video clips to Hasanabi Central's own social media accounts. It is not used by the public and does not collect information about anyone else.

Information the app accesses

When Hasanabi Central's TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • Basic account information (user.info.basic): the account's TikTok ID, display name and profile picture, used to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to post videos (video.publish): used to upload the channel's clips with their captions, and to check whether each upload finished.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, which let the app act for the connected account.

The app does not read followers, messages, comments, viewing history, contacts or any information about other TikTok users.

How it is used

This information is used only to publish Hasanabi Central's videos to Hasanabi Central's own account. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, in a file that only the operator's user account can read. They are not uploaded to any server or database run by us, and they are sent only to TikTok's own API when posting.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with anyone. The only parties the app communicates with are the platforms it posts to (TikTok and Meta).

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account is connected. Revoking the app's access in TikTok (Settings and privacy, then Security, then Apps and services permissions) makes them stop working, and we then delete them. To ask about or request deletion of any data, email us at the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than the channel's operator.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
